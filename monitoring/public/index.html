How to grow the server, step by step

Take one step at a time, and only when the advice above asks for it. For a membership association's website, the first three steps are free and should last for years.

How the protection works

Every visitor may make 30 requests a second (bursts up to 150, for a page with all its pictures). People never get near this. Floods are refused with "too many requests".

Addresses are blocked for an hour (longer if they come back) when they do what no real visitor does: ask for files that don't exist on this site (like .env, .php or wp-login; 8 times in 10 minutes), get the staff password wrong 15 times in 10 minutes, or make 1,500 requests in 5 minutes. This server, private networks and the addresses in ALLOW_IPS are never blocked.

You are warned (here, and on your phone) only when real visitors are affected: the site stops answering, visitors get errors, pages become very slow, or the server is overloaded. An attack that is stopped while visitors are fine is only listed here.

Very large attacks (thousands of computers at once) can fill the server's connection before nginx sees them. If that happens you will be warned, and the fix is Cloudflare's free plan with "Under Attack" mode (see the README).
